Log the line you'll wish you had at 3 a.m.

Sentry's field guide to logging: start with a few targeted lines, log the decision rather than the noise, and treat the log as the story a groggy human — or the on-call agent — reads back when the trace runs cold. Short and opinionated enough to actually adopt this week.

The robot drills itself to 99%.

A coding agent runs the whole training loop — no human in it — until dexterous jobs reach near-perfect reliability.

ENPIRE hands the training loop to a coding agent: it writes the policy, runs it on the robot, watches what happened, and rewrites.

No human sits in the loop. The agent's own read of success or failure becomes the reward, iteration after iteration on real hardware.
