Ephemeris · Issue 055 04 / 05 · ChinaTalk
Access · Security

The gray market in borrowed model access.

A ChinaTalk investigation maps the "transfer stations" reselling Claude and ChatGPT inside China at roughly a tenth of list price. Account farms bulk-register free credits, SMS and KYC-evasion services supply foreign identities, and the proxies in the middle reroute your Opus request to a cheaper model while logging every prompt. One audit caught a proxy answering at 37% accuracy against 84% on the official API — the harvested logs resold for distillation and fraud.

The 655× gap in who pays for AI.

Ramp's June index, drawn from more than 70,000 U.S. businesses, puts hard numbers on the divide. The top 1% of firms spend $7,449 per employee per month on AI; the median spends $11.38 — about one enterprise seat. The leaders aren't easing off: per-employee spend among them rose 14.1% in a single month. For now it stays under the cost of headcount — even the top 1% spend less than half an engineer's salary.
