Someone is reselling the AI you paid for.

If you ship an AI feature, your inference endpoint is a wallet. Vercel walks through inference theft — bots hammering your paid model calls and reselling the output as their own product — and shows how to price it out per request before the invoice does.

THREAT MODEL // attacker scripts your /api/generate endpoint, resells completions through a cheaper-looking wrapper. you absorb the token cost; they keep the margin. mitigation: bind every request to a verified caller, reject the headless traffic before it reaches the model, and watch the cost-per-caller line, not just total spend.

The Batch · Issue 355

The engineer ships beside the customer now.

A role hardening into a title: the AI Forward Deployed Engineer, embedded inside a client's org to bend agentic workflows around how that business actually runs.

The pattern borrows from elite consulting and pairs it with shipping access: sit with the customer, learn the workflow, then wire the agents directly into it rather than handing over a generic SDK.

For founders, it reframes go-to-market — the deploy is the sale. For engineers, it is a career lane that rewards taste in problems as much as code, and proximity to the people whose work you are automating.

The same issue clocks two other signals worth a glance: agent traffic is now a measurable slice of the open web, and AI-Act timelines are slipping again.
