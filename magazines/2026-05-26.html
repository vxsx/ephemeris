Dev tools · SDK

Build your own coding agent over coffee.

The Cursor SDK is a TypeScript package that exposes Agent.create() and agent.send() with configurable models, sandboxed execution, and persistent state. Teams are already wiring it into CI pipelines, internal review bots, and customer-facing flows.

// summarise a PR diff and post the review back
import { Agent } from "@cursor/sdk";

const a = await Agent.create({
  model: "composer-2.5",
  cwd:   process.env.GITHUB_WORKSPACE,
});

const reply = await a.send(
  "Read the diff, flag risky changes, post a CR comment."
);

console.log(reply.text);

Skip seventy percent of attention. Lose nothing.

BLASST drops attention blocks whose softmax score falls below a single scalar threshold. No retraining, no architecture change, no special hardware — just a kernel-level shortcut that earned the MLSys '26 best-paper award.

Paper arXiv:2512.12087 v3 Authors J. Yuan, C. Shinn,
K. Xu, J. Cui,
G. Klimiashvili,
G. Xiao, et al. Venue MLSys 2026 — Best Paper Compatibility Works with DeepSeek MLA. No retraining required.

The technique uses online softmax statistics to identify attention scores that contribute nothing to the output, then skips the corresponding block — both the softmax and the matrix multiply.

At 71.9% block sparsity the authors report a 1.52× speedup on prefill; at 73.2% sparsity, 1.48× on decode. Benchmark accuracy on long-context tasks degrades by less than one percent.

Threshold calibration is automatic and reveals an inverse relationship to context length: the longer the prompt, the more attention you can skip. The kernel is a drop-in for existing inference stacks.

Ephemeris · Issue 038 05 / 06 · Anthropic
AI · Ethics

Anthropic answered the Pope.

Pope Leo XIV's encyclical Magnifica humanitas arrived on 15 May. Ten days later Chris Olah, Anthropic's co-founder, conceded that AI labs work inside incentives that can pull against doing the right thing — and named three places the Church should weigh in.

  1. I. That AI's benefits reach the people whose labour it is most likely to displace — starting with the global poor.
  2. II. That moral imagination keeps pace with capability — what does human flourishing look like when work itself is reshaped?
  3. III. That somebody investigates the mysterious internal states of these models — Olah's word — and what they may amount to.

Research direction

Four questions Anthropic now has a building for.

The Anthropic Institute will publish its own research and data, run a four-month fellowship, and hand its findings to outsiders so they can argue back. The agenda splits four ways.

I Economic Diffusion Where AI lands in the economy, who adopts first, what it does to wages. The Anthropic Economic Index is the running instrument.
II Threats & Resilience Dual-use capabilities, early warning for cyber and bio risk, and the defensive infrastructure society will need to keep up.
III AI in the Wild How people and institutions are actually using these systems — observability, surveys, behavioural shifts at scale.
IV AI-driven R&D When models start training their successors — governance, monitoring, and what counts as an early warning.
