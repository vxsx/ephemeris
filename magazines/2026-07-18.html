The long jobs are where they fold.

Agents · Multi-agent design

The panel that only agrees with itself.

Spin up five sub-agents on the same model and the same context and you get one opinion wearing five name tags. The Council skill names the trap: correlated votes reduce error by roughly nothing, yet the apparent unanimity inflates your confidence. The fix is mandates, not costumes: give each seat a different job and different evidence, then treat disagreement as the only reading worth trusting.

N seats ≈ ONE sample, quoted N times. Persona is decoration. The mandate is the whole thing.

Follow the slow request, span by span.

PostHog's distributed tracing is in beta, and it takes your existing OpenTelemetry exporters — no new SDK to wire in. Errors tell you something broke; logs show scattered events; a trace draws the causality chain, with timing. Because the spans sit next to product events in the warehouse, you can pivot from a 3.2s checkout to the exact N+1 query, then jump straight to that user's session replay.
