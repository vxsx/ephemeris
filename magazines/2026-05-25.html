Adoption · Sales

The chart was a map of distrust, not of capability.

Anthropic published a chart of what AI theoretically can do, by profession, set against what organisations actually deploy it for. Bayram, writing in Products & Startups, argues the blue-to-red gap is not a capability ceiling — it is algorithm aversion. We forgive humans their mistakes and refuse to forgive models theirs.

Five dimensions of distrust
  1. One hallucination collapses confidence the way a hundred human errors do not.
  2. Users want agency, and "let the model decide" reads as the loss of it.
  3. Subjective tasks (partner, lawyer, brand voice) resist automation; objective ones (route, total) accept it.
  4. The question is not "can the agent?" but "will the agent act in my interests?"
  5. Survival anxiety beats learning anxiety; psychological safety is the cheaper lever.

Ephemeris · Issue 037 02 / 7 · Anthropic
Alignment · Method

Claude is reminded to be good — and is.

Anthropic's "widening the conversation" post slipped in a methods nugget. Researchers built a tool that, mid-task, pings Claude with its own ethical commitments. In alignment evals the model showed markedly lower rates of misaligned behaviour. They cannot yet say whether the win comes from the reminder, or from the pause to reflect.

"Markedly lower rates of misaligned behaviour. We are still working out whether the effect is the reminder, or the reflection itself."

v0 opens what it makes.

v0's latest update hands the agent a browser. It opens the app it just generated, navigates the flows it claims to support, captures screenshots, and files bug reports against its own output. The build-test loop closes inside the editor with no human between turns.

v0> ship "checkout flow"
→ generated 14 files in 3.2s
v0> browse --route /checkout
→ opening preview · capturing screenshots
→ /checkout         OK   (200ms)
→ /checkout/pay     OK   (340ms)
→ /checkout/done    FAIL (cart cleared too early)
v0> fix --bug 1 --redeploy
→ patch applied · rerunning browse
→ all routes pass · screenshot diff attached
