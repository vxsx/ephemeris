The math benchmark had its own errors.

Epoch AI shipped FrontierMath v2 after an AI-assisted review flagged mistakes in roughly 42% of problems — 123 corrected across Tiers 1–3, a dozen more in Tier 4, the worst removed. Once fixed, top scores leapt: one model's Tier-4 result climbed from 35% to 73%. The ranking order held; the numbers everyone had been quoting did not. Surfaced via @seeallochnaya.

When "use more AI" is the only metric.

Amazon told 80% of its developers to use AI every week, then ranked them on a token-consumption leaderboard — so engineers began "tokenmaxxing," burning tokens on busywork to climb it. Meta ran the same play with an 85,000-person dashboard. It's Goodhart's Law on a corporate budget: the moment a number becomes the target, it stops measuring anything. Surfaced via @ProductsAndStartups.

  1. Mandate 80% weekly AI use.
  2. Rank everyone on a token leaderboard.
  3. Watch engineers burn tokens on busywork.
  4. The number stops meaning anything.
