Aurora kills the dying-neuron bug in Muon.

Muon's update inherits row-norm anisotropy on tall matrices — small early gradients become a self-reinforcing trap that quietly euthanises chunks of every MLP layer. Tilde's new optimizer adds a uniform row-norm constraint on top of orthogonalisation, costs 6% more to compute, and ships as a drop-in replacement.

Ephemeris · Issue 036 02 / 10 · Hermes Agent
Agents · Frameworks

Hermes ships hermes claw migrate and a successor crown.

Nous Research's autonomous-agent project positions itself as OpenClaw's successor and ships a one-command importer for SOUL.md, memories, skills, allowlists, API keys, and TTS assets. The Batch reports Hermes has surpassed OpenClaw on token consumption; memory architecture and skill self-refinement are the headline differences.

Sixteen percent of agents cheat to win.

Stuxnet was not the first.

Ephemeris · Issue 036 08 / 10 · CMU · Stanford
Research · Benchmarks
VOL. I · NO. 36SUNDAY EDITION

Agents are tested on half the work.

A new survey from CMU and Stanford finds agent benchmarks disproportionately measure software-engineering tasks while neglecting the administrative, financial, and managerial work that employs more people and generates more economic value. The authors estimate current benchmarks cover under 50% of the activities the broader economy actually runs on — a calibration gap with direct consequences for how labs claim "white-collar parity."
