The workhorse, sized three ways.

GPT-5.6 shipped July 9 as a family, not a model: Sol for hard coding and agents, Terra for the middle, Luna tuned for latency. Sol tops the independent Coding Agent Index at 80 and hits 88.8% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 — above GPT-5.5's 83.4% — and it's already the default across Codex, the API, and Copilot.

Video, billed by the second.

Google put two media models in the Gemini API. Omni Flash makes 10-second clips at $0.10 a second, taking text, images, and up to three seconds of reference video; Nano Banana 2 Lite returns a 1K image in about four seconds for $0.034 per thousand. Both are reachable from AI Studio, Flow, or a single call.

The tell is in your tremor.

Precursor injects an obfuscated script that watches a whole session — pointer paths, keystroke rhythm, focus, visibility — instead of a single checkpoint. It scores the human tells bots can't fake at scale: wrist-pivot geometry, cognitive-load delays, the micro-tremor of a real hand. A page refresh no longer resets the signature. Keystrokes are read as timing, never the keys themselves.

Twenty-five ideas, six that count.

A new Anthropic paper maps "J-space" — the concepts a model can actually verbalize — with a Jacobian Lens that measures which internal features steer the output. Zvi's read: about 25 concepts sit there at once, but only ~6 are truly distinct. Swap one ("France" → "China") and its capital, language, and currency shift with it. Automatic, System-1 answers skip the space entirely.
