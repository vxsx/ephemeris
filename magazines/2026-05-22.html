The mutex ate the billing pipeline.

Cloudflare repartitioned its billing ClickHouse cluster by (namespace, day) — and watched query latency balloon. Flame‑graphing real threads pinned the leak to a single mutex over the parts list, eating more than half of every query. Shared locks, a cached parts list, and binary partition search dropped duration by 50 % and broke the link between part count and latency.

What the lull is hiding.

Even quiet weeks in AI now ship new knowledge — and a new kind of slop to bury it under.

New knowledge, real

Zvi reads OpenAI's unit‑distance proof as actual mathematical discovery: an 80‑year conjecture disproved, with constructions independent mathematicians can verify. Gowers called it a milestone for AI in mathematics.

New slop, also real

The same week brought the discovery that prize‑contest pipelines are now thick with AI‑generated submissions. Quality‑filtered channels are starting to fail in ways that affect the people running them.

Coordination, missing

The deeper worry is structural: frontier labs face competitive pressure that makes restraint individually irrational, and corporate framings of that fact carry no urgency about alternatives. Nate Soares's critique runs through it — claiming the "good guy" mantle while accelerating toward systems you cannot control is itself a failure of responsibility.
