Ephemeris · Issue 043 03 / 05 · OpenAI
Health AI · Case study

Forty rare diagnoses the workup missed.

Boston Children's put OpenAI's models to work across diagnostics and back-office load — and the practical win looks like triage, not magic.

Clinicians credit the system with surfacing more than forty rare-disease cases the standard workup had not flagged, by reading across notes, labs and imaging that no single specialist reviews together.

The hospital frames it as decision support: the model proposes candidates and the evidence behind them, and a physician confirms. The reported gains are in time-to-diagnosis and in catching the long-tail conditions that hide in plain sight.
