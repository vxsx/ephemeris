The route says it came from you. It didn't.

Forged-origin BGP hijacks strip the attacker's own network out of the path and impersonate someone legitimate. Cloudflare makes the case for "First AS" enforcement — a router check that the leftmost hop in an announcement matches the peer that actually sent it, and a treat-as-withdraw when it doesn't. Their measurements found 7 of 14 Tier-1 backbones enforce it by default; the other 7 will still carry a forged path.

Ephemeris · Issue 047 05 / 05 · PostHog
Databases · Data Engineering

Pick by the workload, not the logo.

PostHog's Mathew Pregasen lays out a comparison worth bookmarking. Postgres is a row-based, client-server OLTP database built for transactions and concurrent writers; DuckDB is an embedded, vectorized OLAP engine built for analytical scans. Past ~100M rows, Postgres strains on aggregations where DuckDB — batching thousands of values at a time — accelerates. They solve different problems, and the value here is drawing that line cleanly.
