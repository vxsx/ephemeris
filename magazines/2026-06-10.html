Enterprise AI · Strategy

The bottleneck is context, not the model.

In a Stanford MS&E 435 lecture, Yash Patil of Applied Compute makes the case that the hard part of enterprise AI is not the base model — it's wiring in the knowledge a company already holds. Specialised, domain-tuned models routinely beat a stronger generic one once that internal context is in reach.

Lecture notes · enterprise knowledge

"A weaker model that can see your data beats a stronger one that can't."

The work that moves the needle sits upstream of the prompt: extracting tacit process knowledge, resolving which of three conflicting internal docs is current, and feeding that into retrieval an agent can trust.

The takeaway for a founder or staff engineer is to spend the next quarter on the context pipeline — sources, freshness, access — before reaching for a bigger model.
