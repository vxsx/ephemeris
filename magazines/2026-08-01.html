The early exit was costing 15×.

GitHub's code search lowercases every byte it indexes. The obvious loop breaks out at the first non-ASCII byte — and that break is exactly what stops the compiler from emitting vector instructions. Alexander Neubeck and Greg Orzell deleted it. Sweeping the whole buffer unconditionally runs at memory bandwidth; stopping early runs at a fifteenth of it.

ascii · branch-free sweep45.0 GiB/s
ascii · with early exit3.0 GiB/s
pure CJK2.95 GiB/s
unicode · worst case869 MiB/s
full simple-fold table1,776 bytes

The rest is byte-space arithmetic — folding inside UTF-8 without decoding and re-encoding — plus a paged bitmap that fits every Unicode simple fold in under 2 KB. Keeping the break and making the body perfectly branch-free still gets you zero vector instructions. The branch has to go.

One POST request buys you a global relay.

Media over QUIC relays are now provisionable. Call the API, get an anycast relay live across 330+ cities in seconds, and hand out separate JWT credentials for publishers and subscribers — each scoped, each with its own expiry, each revocable on its own.

Bearer token. No region choice, no capacity planning.

Sixty hours of model time halved a post-quantum key.

Claude Mythos Preview found a nontrivial automorphism in HAWK's lattice that nobody had published, then built it into an improved key-recovery attack. The scheme's authors would need to double key sizes to hold the security level they advertised. A second effort produced a 200–800× faster meet-in-the-middle attack on 7-round AES-128 using a fingerprinting trick the team named the Möbius Bridge.

Ephemeris · Issue 102 04 / 05 · Pangram · via Don't Worry About the Vase
AI tools · Provenance

One real photo in 625 comes back marked fake.

Pangram Image reads output from DALL·E, Gemini, Midjourney, FLUX and Grok Imagine, and it catches frames lifted out of Kling and Veo. That false-positive figure — 0.16% on ReLAION — is the number to argue with before you wire it into anything that accuses a person of something.

Known limits

  • Anything under 512×512 px is refused outright.
  • Deepfakes and face swaps are not detected with confidence.
  • An AI-edited photograph can read as fully synthetic.
  • NSFW content is not processed.
  • Web dashboard is open to free and paid accounts; API access is invitation-only during the research preview.
