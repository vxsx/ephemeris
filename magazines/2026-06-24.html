The clock on encryption starts now.

A new executive order puts hard dates on post-quantum migration — a named lead by July, plans by September, encryption moved by end of 2030 and authentication by 2031, contractors included. Cloudflare's counsel: stop waiting for a perfect crypto inventory. Turn on post-quantum encryption for internet-facing traffic today, write it into procurement at no extra cost, and start a quantum-impact inventory for the long-lived keys that "harvest now, decrypt later" attacks come for first.

Everyone fishes rationally. The ocean dies anyway.

Fish Banks rebuilds Dennis Meadows' tragedy-of-the-commons model as a playable multiplayer sandbox. Each team sees only its own cash and catch, never the hidden fish stock, so individually sensible buying strip-mines the shared sea. It runs fully offline, headless from Node, and is built for an agent to extend — add quotas, change pricing, or let Claude run a fishing company and watch it drain the commons. What it drills home is that structure, not character, drives the crash.
