An export order suspends Fable 5 and Mythos 5.

On 12 June the US government invoked national-security authorities and ordered Anthropic to cut off Fable 5 and Mythos 5 for every user — citizens and foreign-national staff alike. The cited cause is a jailbreak that bypasses the models' safeguards; Anthropic calls the demonstrated vulnerability narrow and widely available from other models, and complied while disagreeing in public.

"Applying this standard would essentially halt all new model deployments for all frontier model providers."

— Anthropic, on the directive · filed 12 Jun 2026

Ephemeris · Issue 058 04 / 6 · The Batch · DeepLearning.AI
Research · Model bias

Ask in Chinese, get a friendlier government.

A study surfaced in The Batch — and published in Nature — finds that state-controlled media in training data tilts a model's output, and the tilt tracks the language of the prompt. The practical read for anyone shipping on these models: a model's stance can move with the language you query it in.

Field note · language-dependent stance

The finding. Researchers from Oregon, Purdue, UC San Diego, NYU and Princeton tested identical political questions across languages and measured how favorably models described each country's government.

The tilt. Prompted in Chinese, models described China's government markedly more favorably than when the same questions were asked in English — an artifact of state media in the training corpus.

The takeaway. Press-freedom of a language's source media leaks into model behavior. If your product serves multiple languages, audit the answer, not just the translation.

Your CI pipeline can now run an agent.

GitHub Agentic Workflows is in public preview: Actions can drive an autonomous AI agent as a workflow step. The same pipeline that builds and tests can dispatch an agent to triage an issue, reproduce a bug, or open a PR. The automation layer you already own gets a brain.

$ cat .github/workflows/triage.yml
# public preview · Actions + autonomous agent
on: issues
jobs:
  triage:
    uses: github/agentic-workflow
    with:
      task: "label, reproduce, propose a fix"
