Strip the stray header before it drags your cache cold.

A single Set-Cookie or wrong Cache-Control from your origin is enough to make a perfectly cacheable asset uncacheable. Cache Response Rules add a new stage — after the origin answers, before Cloudflare stores — where you rewrite those headers without touching origin code.
