Ephemeris · Issue 061 05 / 06 · OpenAI
Benchmarks · Research

Grading models on the lab work scientists actually do.

OpenAI released LifeSciBench, an evaluation for how AI systems handle real-world life-science research tasks rather than textbook recall.

LifeSciBench Expert-authored Expert-reviewed Real research tasks

The benchmark targets the messy, multi-step problems a working scientist faces — protocol design, interpretation, the judgment calls between steps — not multiple-choice trivia a model can pattern-match.

If you build evals, the method is the lesson: domain-grounded grading where subject-matter experts both write the questions and check the answers, so a high score means competence rather than recall.
