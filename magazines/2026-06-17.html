A Stanford lecture with Baseten's Tuhin Srivastava reframes inference as economics: GPU utilization, heterogeneous compute, and the widening gap between open-weight models you host and frontier models you rent. For anyone shipping AI features, unit cost is a design constraint, not a footnote.
