Frontier coding, open weights, one-tenth the bill.

MiniMax M3 (June 1) scores 59% on SWE-Bench Pro — past GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro, just behind Opus 4.7 — while a new sparse-attention design cuts compute roughly 20× and runs input about 9× faster. Weights and a tech report land within ten days on Hugging Face and GitHub, for private clusters and fine-tuning. Input runs near a tenth the price of Opus 4.7 or GPT-5.5 — the gap that compounds across an agent loop.

Ephemeris · Issue 048 02 / 06 · Biohub
Science · Protein Design

Protein binders, designed in days.

Biohub's ESMFold2 (May 27) turns the evolutionary signal in its ESMC language model into atomic-resolution structure — and beats AlphaFold 3 on the hard, therapeutically central problem of antibody–antigen folding. A looped-transformer design lets it spend more compute at inference and keep improving. The practical shift: early binder discovery moves from empirical screening that ran for months or years to computation-guided design measured in days.

Ephemeris · Issue 048 03 / 06 · Cloudflare
Dev Tools · JS Toolchain

The toolchain under every front end finds a new home.

VoidZero — the team behind Vite, Vitest, Rolldown, the Oxc parser and the Vite+ toolchain — is joining Cloudflare (June 4). Vite alone pulls about 129 million weekly downloads and sits under Vue, React Router, Angular and more, so the real question is who funds it. Cloudflare's answer: keep it all MIT-licensed, vendor-neutral and community-run, and seed a $1M Vite ecosystem fund administered by the core team. Nothing in your build changes today.

What 832 banned accounts did with AI.

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team studied 832 accounts it banned for malicious cyber activity between March 2025 and March 2026, mapping each to MITRE ATT&CK. The change is in where AI shows up — not just initial access but the later, complex stages of an intrusion. Their caution for defenders: skill level and technique count no longer track how dangerous an actor is.
