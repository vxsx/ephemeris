Ephemeris · Issue 035 01 / 07 · Anthropic Research
Security · Anthropic

Finding bugs is now the easy part.

Project Glasswing put Claude Mythos against a thousand open-source projects and forty-eight partner products for one month. The model returned ten thousand high or critical findings. Mozilla alone got two hundred seventy-one new Firefox bugs. The disclosure queue is now the bottleneck — average patch time runs two weeks per bug, and seventy-five of five hundred thirty reported flaws have shipped a fix.

Claude could do 94% of the job. It does 33%.

Anthropic's new "observed exposure" measure crosses theoretical capability with what Claude.ai is actually used for. The gap is wide everywhere, but widest in Computer & Math: theoretical coverage runs to 94% of tasks, real-world usage to a third. The bottleneck isn't whether the model can do it — it's trust, software integration, and verification workflows that haven't been built.

Benchmarks · CMU & Stanford

Forty-three percent of work is not on the bench.

Carnegie Mellon and Stanford mapped today's leading agent benchmarks to O*NET's labor-statistics taxonomy. The benchmarks cover 56.5% of US work activities; the missing 43.5% is mostly the economy. Software-engineering tasks dominate by an order of magnitude, while administrative support and management — the largest categories of US employment — barely register.

Ephemeris · Issue 035 04 / 07 · Sentry Engineering
Observability · Sentry

The agent shipped your app. without seams.

A coding agent writes a Next.js front end, a Supabase Edge Function, a Postgres query, and ships before lunch. None of it has tracing. Sentry's piece walks through wiring a single trace through all three layers so the N+1 query the agent quietly introduced becomes visible. The trick that makes the AI assistant useful afterwards is the MCP server — not its training data.

sentry.client.ts · next 16.2 · supabase 2.x ● ● ● 
// One init wires browser, edge function, and Postgres into one trace.
$ Sentry.init({
    dsn: process.env.SENTRY_DSN,
    tracesSampleRate: 0.1,
    integrations: [
      Sentry.supabaseIntegration(supabaseClient, Sentry, {
        tracing: true,
        breadcrumbs: true,
      }),
    ],
  });

// Now the agent's hidden N+1 shows up under perf → slow transactions.
$ sentry mcp install --client cursor   # for the *next* fix

An AI agent gets underwritten for the first time.

ElevenLabs paired its voice-agent platform with the new Artificial Intelligence Underwriting Company to issue the first AIUC-1 insurance certification.
