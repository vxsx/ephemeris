Research · Labor Benchmark

Machines now finish one job in six.

The Remote Labor Index scores how often an agent completes real freelance work — design, CAD, video — judged against a professional's output. Fable 5 clears 16.1%, up from 2.5% eight months ago. The number is small; the slope is not.

16.1%Fable 5
8.3%Opus 4.8
6.3%GPT-5.5

2.5% → 16.1% in under eight months. Caveat: automated graders overstate the win — only human review holds the line.

Ephemeris · Issue 073 03 / 6 · Cloudflare
Web · Payments

The web grows a cash register.

Cloudflare's Monetization Gateway lets you charge for pages, APIs, datasets and MCP tools using the x402 protocol and stablecoins. Payment is verified at the edge, so micropayments down to fractions of a cent settle in under a second — priced for a reader that is now a bot.

GET /dataset.parquet
← 402 Payment Required
  accept: x402 · USDC
  price : $0.0004 / call
→ pay + retry
← 200 OK · settled 380ms

Read the trace your eval can't.

Reading individual traces exposes fabrications that sail past tool-call validation — a made-up speaker name that looks grounded. Log with stable IDs; inspect tool output, not just the call.

A biology exam top models fail.

GeneBench-Pro hands a model a dataset, an experimental context and a research question, then asks it to pick a method and reach a defensible conclusion — 129 synthetic problems with verifiable ground truth. The best system clears barely a third; most real biology still goes unanswered.

One scale for how bad a jailbreak is.

Anthropic, Amazon, Microsoft and Google agreed a consensus way to score a jailbreak instead of arguing about it. Four axes feed a severity tier — from a break inside safety margins to a universal one that unlocks whole classes of harm — so labs and regulators can react to the same number.
