Ephemeris · Issue 068 03 / 06 · Apple ML Research
On-device AI · Research

The on-device model that loads only the weights it needs.

Apple's third-generation Foundation Models put two models on the phone: a 3B dense Core and a 20B sparse Core Advanced that activates just one to four billion parameters per request. Instruction-Following Pruning keeps the full model in flash and pulls only the needed weights into RAM; Quantization-Aware Training compresses the rest without losing accuracy. The recipe is how a 20B model fits where a 3B one used to.

20B params

…of which only 1–4B activate on any given request — sparse by design, flash-resident by default.

A swing at the common cold, fully funded.

Intercept is a $500M nonprofit — backed by Stripe, the OpenAI Foundation, Anthropic, and Bill Gates — taking on colds and flu from two directions. One is broad-spectrum prevention: vaccines, pills, and nasal sprays that hit many respiratory viruses at once. The other is the room itself: filtration, far-UVC, and antimicrobial vapours to cut transmission in shared spaces. It runs on a four-to-seven-year research horizon.
